Apply the confirmed-identity investigation gate on testing/enterprise-readiness.

Required behavior:
- /api/vendors/resolve issues a short-lived opaque authorization only for CONFIRMED identity.
- /api/investigate requires and consumes the single-use authorization.
- invalid, expired, replayed, mismatched, or non-confirmed identity cannot create a job.
- authorization tokens never appear in job state, reports, logs, or artifacts.
- the orchestrator receives only the sanitized confirmed identity snapshot.
